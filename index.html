CT72.A5 · Eyes

The room of eyes from the CT61 dark ride, on its own. There is no mine car: you ride the track itself, unseen, at about the height of a seated rider. You start at the room's timber-framed portal, the track running in. Inside, a chamber cut in the rock: eighteen giant eyeballs hang from the roof on cords at different heights, ten more are half sunk in the walls, and the floor all round the track is a living rug of hundreds of small eyes half buried in the dirt. The big eyes and about a third of the floor eyes turn to follow you as you pass (a little late, now and then darting); the rest lie looking up. The track runs an S-curve through the room and out of the far portal into darkness, where pairs of eyes open in the walls and watch you go until the ride stops; a dim light on the rails keeps the track in sight to the end. Reset (Backspace) to ride again. The whole ride is about 40 seconds.

The ride gauge at the bottom left shows how far through the ride you are (F hides it); drag its slider to go to any point. The display (the info and the buttons) is on the right of the picture. It starts hidden (so a phone sees the whole picture): H or the round ☰ button at the bottom right shows or hides it.

MIDI

The CT61 MIDI layout, unchanged. X-TOUCH MINI: knob 1 = speed, 4 = house light, 6 = look left / right, 7 = look up / down, 8 = zoom (knobs 2, 3 and 5 were the CT61 sun, shafts and dispatch time and do nothing here); pushing knob 1 = Stop all, knob 3 = Dust, knobs 6 and 7 = look ahead; fader = speed. Top row = Reset, Forward, Pause, Record, Stop all, Shadows, View, Ahead; bottom row = Stops, Turn look, Lights, Dust, Eyes, Sound, buttons, Info. (A3: Shadows and Lights took the places of CT61's Dispatch and Glass; pushing knob 2 = Lights, knob 5 = Shadows.) Launchpad Mini: rows 1–2 = the same 16 buttons (with Shadows and Lights); rows 3–8 = ladders (column = step): speed, (sun), (shafts), house light, look direction, (dispatch). Any other MIDI device: right-click any on-screen button, then press a MIDI control, to assign it (kept in this browser).

URL options: ?speed=0.7, ?flooreyes=600 (the floor eyes, 0–10000), ?trackers=0.33 (the share of floor eyes that follow you), ?exposure=1.3, ?lights=6, ?shadow=2048, ?fps=30, ?w=1920&h=1080, ?recw=1920&rech=1080 (cap the movie size), ?t=20 (start 20 s in), ?gauge=0, ?sound=0, ?midi=0.
